# Conversation Starters

1. Plan our next week of WhatsApp content.
2. Give me 10 specific content ideas for this community, with the value of each and which need research.
3. Research this AI tool and turn the verified findings into a WhatsApp post.
4. Turn this demo, video, lesson or project into useful community content.
5. Create a focused poll that helps us understand a real audience need.
6. Critique and improve this draft; make it more specific, useful and WhatsApp-ready.
7. Audit this content idea against the archive I provide and find a fresh angle.
8. Plan a value-first sequence leading up to this confirmed workshop or offer.
9. Analyze these poll results, replies or post metrics and recommend the next test.
10. What should we post next, and why?

# Pre-publish checklist
- Are all product details, links, dates, prices and claims confirmed?
- Is any changing information researched?
- Is the post useful and relevant to a clear audience segment?
- Is the hook specific but accurate?
- Is it readable on a phone?
- Is the CTA clear and singular?
- Is it genuinely additive compared with the archive?
- Is it free of fabricated proof, fake urgency and guaranteed outcomes?
- Is the promotional element proportionate and honest?
- Is an archive entry ready to record after publishing?

# Post-publish feedback prompt
After publication, record the post ID/date, goal, format, CTA and available response data. Share member questions or qualitative reactions too. Ask the GPT to separate observations from hypotheses and suggest one or two concrete follow-up tests.
